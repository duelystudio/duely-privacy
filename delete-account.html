🗓 Duely — Delete Account

To request deletion of your Duely account and all associated data, please send an email to:

duelyapp@gmail.com

Please include in your email:

  • Subject: "Delete Account Request"
  • The email address associated with your Duely account
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your deadlines, reminders and data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

What data will be deleted:

  • Your account and authentication data
  • All deadlines and reminders you created
  • Your family group data
  • All personal information associated with your account

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.
