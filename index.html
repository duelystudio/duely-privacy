🗓 Duely - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Introduction

Duely ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you sign in with Google or create an account.
  • App data: Deadlines, subscriptions, and reminders you create within the app.
  • Device information: Basic device information for app functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app service
  • Send you notifications and reminders you set up
  • Enable family group sharing features
  • Improve the app experience

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers located in Europe (Frankfurt, Germany). All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication and data storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is only shared with family group members you explicitly invite within the app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Duely is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data

10. Advertising

Duely uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: duelyapp@outlook.it

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
